**What the fine print actually says:**

A few policy details that affect real money, all confirmed on the official site:

- **30-day money-back guarantee.** Full refund within 30 days, subject to the Terms of Service, requested through the refund page in your client area. That gives you a low-risk window to test the routing that matters to you.
- **No bandwidth overage charges.** If you hit your monthly transfer cap, the VPS is suspended until the next cycle. No surprise invoice. Annoying if it happens mid-month, but infinitely better than a $200 overage bill — and you can fix it immediately by upgrading or resetting traffic.
- **Free migration between locations.** On multi-location plans (all KVM promo plans and the CN2 GIA-E line), you can move your VPS between eligible datacenters from the KiwiVM panel anytime, without data loss. If DC6 feels congested, you can try DC9 or JPOS_1 at no cost.
- **Self-managed, emphatically.** You get root access and a control panel, not hand-holding. If you've never administered a Linux server, the learning curve is real and the support team won't configure your applications for you.
- **No stored payment credentials.** BandwagonHost doesn't keep your card on file and charge automatically; renewals happen when you decide to pay again. Good for control, slightly risky if you're the forgetful type with an annual plan.
- **Uptime commitments.** Standard promo plans list 99.95%; the SLA line is contractually 99.99%.
- **Mainland access note.** The main site is reported to be blocked from mainland China; the company operates numbered mirror domains (covered in every recent third-party guide) for buyers there.
